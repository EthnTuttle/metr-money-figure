Both TIME100 AI profiles of METR's CEO were written by a fellow of a journalism program that Coefficient funds; neither told readers so

The chain: Coefficient Giving, the funder behind METR's parent, partners and pooled funds, gave $6.29M to the Tarbell Center, which places fellows at outlets including TIME. Tarbell fellow Harry Booth wrote TIME's 2024 and 2026 TIME100 AI profiles of Beth Barnes; six of nine METR articles at three outlets were by Tarbell fellows. None carried a disclosure line. Tarbell says its donors have no editorial control, and nothing here says the profiles are wrong.
