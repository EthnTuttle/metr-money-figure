Constellation Institute

We are a non-profit working to ensure transformative AI is developed, deployed, and governed safely

Advanced AI may drive unprecedented human prosperity.

But it also brings the risk of global catastrophe.
Permanent loss of control
of human civilization to AIs
Extreme mass casualty events
or human extinction
Lock-in of large-scale authoritarian regimes

Constellation Institute reduces these risks by developing talent, supporting key players, and creating space for coordination.

Our four strategies:
1
Mobilization
Identifying and placing talent in high-impact roles across the field.
2
Incubation
Giving founders the support to launch new organizations and projects.
3
Growth
Helping organizations scale their impact and fulfill their missions.
4
Convening
Facilitating collaboration and coordination across the AI safety field.

We run an independent research center in Berkeley, CA where exceptional researchers, forecasters, and operators collaborate on the hardest problems in AI safety and security.
