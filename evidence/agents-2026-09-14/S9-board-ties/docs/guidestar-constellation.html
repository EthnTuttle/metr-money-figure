What are the organization's current programs, how do they measure success, and who do the programs serve?

Program 1

CONSTELLATION COORDINATES, EMPOWERS AND ENGAGES IN RESEARCH, ANALYSIS, AND EDUCATION AIMED AT REDUCING RISKS FROM TRANSFORMATIVE TECHNOLOGIES AND SAFEGUARDING THE FUTURE. CONSTELLATION BEGAN OPERATIONS IN 2023 AND FACILITATED RESEARCH THROUGH ITS RESEARCH CENTER WHICH IS A HUB FOR RESEARCHERS, SCIENTISTS, ENGINEERS AND OTHER RELEVANT PROFESSIONS TO WORK AND COLLABORATE ON AI MISSION-ALIGNED PROJECTS. THE CENTER SUPPORTS INTERNAL RESEARCH CONDUCTED BY THE ORGANIZATION AND EXTERNAL RESEARCH AND PROJECTS BY OTHER ORGANIZATIONS. THE ORGANIZATION ALSO PLANS TO HOST EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMMING AND TRAINING OPPORTUNITIES RELATING TO AI SAFETY.
