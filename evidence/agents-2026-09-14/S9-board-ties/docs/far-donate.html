Support our mission

Invest in a future where AI benefits everyone

At FAR.AI, we're dedicated to ensuring that advanced AI is safe and beneficial for everyone. Your support can make a profound difference in this critical field. By donating to FAR.AI, you're not just contributing funds, you're investing in the future of humanity.

Maximize your impact

Your donation will directly support pioneering research and initiatives that address the most pressing challenges in AI safety. Our work focuses on delivering technical breakthroughs that have the potential to safeguard the future as AI systems become more integrated into society.

Recognized excellence

Our efforts have been endorsed by reputable organizations dedicated to effective philanthropy, such as Founders Pledge, Coefficient Giving (previously Open Philanthropy), and Longview Philanthropy. These endorsements highlight the effectiveness of our work towards developing trustworthy & secure AI.

Transparent and accountable

We are committed to transparency and accountability. We provide regular updates on the tangible impact of your contributions and all research is made publicly available with source code open-sourced. Your trust is paramount, and we ensure that your donation is utilized effectively to advance our mission.

Let's Talk About Impact

Ways to support us

Make a donation

Every contribution, no matter the size, brings us one step closer to a safer AI future. Donating, even a small amount, sends a powerful signal to our partners that people care about AI safety enough to contribute their own money to the cause.

Connect with us

For significant contributions or to discuss how your donation can make the greatest impact, we'd love to speak with you directly.

Let's talk about impact

As AI capabilities advance rapidly, the window to ensure their safe integration into society is narrowing. Your support can help us:

Accelerate research

Expand our in-house research efforts to develop solutions that mitigate AI risks.

Empower the community

Enhance our programs like FAR.Labs and grantmaking initiatives to support more researchers in the field.

Influence global policy

Extend our events and dialogues to shape international policies on AI safety.

We understand that deciding where to donate is a significant decision. We invite you to speak with us to learn more about our work, our impact, and how your support can make a difference. By engaging with us directly, we can explore the counterfactual impact of your donation — what can be achieved with your support that might not happen otherwise.
