Case Studies

Ada, the leader in AI customer service, partnered with AIUC to raise the bar for AI trust, safety, and reliability. By certifying against the AIUC-1 framework, Ada went through the most rigorous testing standards --- far exceeding the typical enterprise requirements. This enabled one of the largest social media platforms to confidently use Ada's generative AI agents to elevate their customer experience and advance their mission of becoming the safest and most positive place on the internet.

Intercom, trusted by nearly 30,000 customer service leaders, partnered with AIUC to prove that customer-facing AI can meet the same technical rigor as traditional enterprise software. Through the AIUC-1 certification, Intercom validated Fin, its AI agent, to test its resilience against real-world threats - from jailbreaks and prompt injections to data leakage - complementing its ISO 42001 governance with detailed technical assurance rooted in thousands of technical tests. The result: security conversations were transformed from a procurement blocker into a competitive differentiator.
