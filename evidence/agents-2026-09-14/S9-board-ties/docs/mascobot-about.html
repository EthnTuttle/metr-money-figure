About

Welcome to my little corner of the internet. I like to build things, train AI models, build robots, and help fellow founders.

I am interested in large-scale AI models, mechanistic interpretability, and robotics.

I am a roboticist currently researching AI models for interpretability, and I am back into robotics too. Previously, I was a Partner at a16z, where I invested and worked very closely with AI and software infra companies. With a16z, I invested and worked with Cursor, Thinking Machines, Black Forest Labs (FLUX models, from the original Stable Diffusion team), Waveforms AI, Deeptune (RL Envs), etc. Independently, I have invested in a few more.

Given my longstanding interest in robotics, over a decade ago I cofounded Fellow AI (fka Fellow Robots), an AI and robotics company based in the San Francisco Bay Area, with former NASA Astronaut Dan Barry, where we built and deployed autonomous robots for retail stores, warehouses, and factories. In 2014, we built and launched LoweBot, Oshbot, and cobot robots with major retailers, datacenters, and factories.

I spent some time in Japan doing AI and robotics research related to the Fukushima nuclear disaster response and working on autonomous wheelchair robots for people with disabilities.

I also joined Singularity University's faculty at the NASA Campus in Silicon Valley, where I was an AI and Robotics Teaching Fellow (2012–2014), working with Ray Kurzweil and Peter Diamandis to research and study the rapid advancements of AI.

Before that, I worked at BMW in Munich on robotics and new vehicle design (some of my designs appear in the MINI Cooper 2014 series).

I started programming in Assembler and C when I was ~12 years old, after I learned about (and loved) the Microchip PIC microcontrollers. I have been building robots since I was a kid and later I joined FIRST Robotics and multiple robotics competitions. I started working with SLAM (Lidar based), V-SLAM, and CNNs around the AlexNet era (~2012).
