## Who we are

Team

We are an AI agent standards & insurance company based in San Francisco.

Our team brings together experience across AI risk and insurance — having spent time at Anthropic, McKinsey insurance, METR, and Center for AI Safety.

We raised a $15M seed round led by Nat Friedman. Investors include Emergence, Terrain, an Anthropic co-founder, and former Chief Information Security Officers at Google Cloud and MongoDB.
