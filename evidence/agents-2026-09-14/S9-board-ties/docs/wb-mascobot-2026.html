About

Welcome to my little corner of the internet. I like to build things, train AI models, build robots, and help fellow founders.

I am interested in large-scale AI models, new model capabilities and architectures, reinforcement learning, and robotics.

I am a Partner at a16z, where I invest in AI and software infra companies. With a16z, I have invested in Cursor, Thinking Machines, Black Forest Labs, Waveforms AI, Deeptune, etc., and angel invested in a few more.

Given our longstanding interest in robotics, I cofounded Fellow AI (over a decade ago!), an AI and robotics company based in the San Francisco Bay Area, with former NASA Astronaut Dan Barry, optimizing supply chains in brick-and-mortar retail stores, warehouses, and factories with autonomous robots; we built and launched in 2014 LoweBot, Oshbot, and Cobot robots with major retailers.

I spent some time in Japan doing AI and robotics research for the Fukushima nuclear accident and working on autonomous wheelchair robots for people with disabilities.

I also joined Singularity University's faculty at the NASA Campus in Silicon Valley, where I was an AI and Robotics Teaching Fellow (2012~2014), working with Ray Kurzweil and Peter Diamandis to research and study the rapid advancements of AI.

Before that, I worked at BMW in Munich on robotics and new vehicle design (some of my designs appear in the MINI Cooper 2014 series).

I started programming in Assembler and C when I was ~12 years old, after I learned about (and loved) the Microchip PIC microcontrollers. I have been building robots since I was a kid and later I joined FIRST Robotics and multiple robotics competitions. I started working with SLAM (Lidar based), V-SLAM, and CNNs around the AlexNet era (~2012).
