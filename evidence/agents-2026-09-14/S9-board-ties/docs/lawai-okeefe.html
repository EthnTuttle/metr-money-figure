Cullen O’Keefe

Managing Director of Research
Area of Expertise:
Legal Frontiers US Law & Policy AI Agents International & Comparative Governance National Security & Strategic Stability Constitutional & Administrative Law Rights, Fairness & Privacy Corporate & Market Regulation

Cullen is the Managing Director of Research at LawAI, where he also leads the Legal Frontiers team. Prior to LawAI, Cullen worked at OpenAI in various legal and policy research roles. Cullen is also a Research Affiliate at GovAI. Cullen graduated with a JD cum laude from Harvard Law School in 2019.
