**Craft Ventures LLC** is an American [venture capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venture_capital "Venture capital") firm based in San Francisco which makes early stage venture investments. Craft was founded in 2017 by [David O. Sacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_O._Sacks "David O. Sacks") and Bill Lee.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-Axios_2018-1)

Craft was founded in 2017 by David O. Sacks, formerly of [Yammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yammer "Yammer") and [PayPal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PayPal "PayPal")[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-WSJ1018-2) and Bill Lee,[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-Axios_2017-3)[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-Jackson_2018-4) formerly of Remarq and Big Fish Games. [Jeff Fluhr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Fluhr "Jeff Fluhr"),[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-Bloomberg-5) founder and former CEO of [StubHub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StubHub "StubHub"), joined as a general partner in 2018 and [Sky Dayton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Dayton "Sky Dayton"), founder of [EarthLink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EarthLink "EarthLink") and [Boingo Wireless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boingo_Wireless "Boingo Wireless"), joined as venture partner the same year.

Craft announced its first fund of $350 million in January 2018[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-This_Week_in_Startups-6) and its second fund of $500M in October 2019.[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-TechCrunch-7)

Craft Ventures I ($350 million) was raised in 2017.[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-8)[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-9)

Craft Ventures II ($500 million) was raised in 2019.[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-10)

In August 2021, Craft announced the closing of Craft Ventures III ($612 million) and Craft Ventures Growth I ($510 million), focusing on [SaaS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SaaS "SaaS") businesses and [marketplace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marketplace "Marketplace") businesses.[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-11)[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-12)

In November 2023, Craft announced the closing of Craft Ventures IV ($712 million) and Craft Ventures Growth II ($608 million).[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craft_Ventures#cite_note-13)
