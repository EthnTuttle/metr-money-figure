# Elon Musk raises $6 billion to challenge OpenAI

Elon Musk's AI startup — xAI — [announced](https://x.ai/blog/series-b) that it has raised $6 billion in one of the largest venture capital funding rounds of all time.

**Why it matters:**This could help Musk begin to catch up with ChatGPT maker OpenAI, which he co-founded before leaving in a dispute that's [become litigious](https://www.axios.com/2024/03/06/openai-snaps-elon-musk-lawsuit).

**The intrigue:**Some of the xAI investors, such as Andreessen Horowitz and Sequoia Capital, also backed OpenAI.

**Zoom in:**All of the $6 billion is new money, Musk tells Axios, rather than shares ["given" to investors](https://www.axios.com/2023/12/06/elon-musk-twitter-x-ai-funding) in Musk's takeover of Twitter/X.

*   That said, there's plenty of overlap in the investor list, and it's unclear if the original Twitter/X backers also received additional equity in xAI, which [Musk says](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1794976923317149914) is now valued at $24 billion.
*   In addition to Andreessen and Sequoia, investors in the Series B round include Valor Equity Partners, Vy Capital, Fidelity, Craft Ventures, Prince Alwaleed Bin Talal and Kingdom Holding.

**The bottom line:**xAI didn't say much about what the new money will go toward, but The Information [recently reported](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/musk-plans-xai-supercomputer-dubbed-gigafactory-of-compute) that it plans to build a massive new supercomputer — the "Gigafactory of compute" —possibly in partnership with Oracle.
