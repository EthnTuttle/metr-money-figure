# What AI conflicts? Silicon Valley elites say David Sacks is just doing his job

A New York Times investigation flagged conflicts in the AI czar’s holdings. The tech world rolled its eyes and rallied to his defense.

David Sacks is helping to shape the nation’s AI policy while holding stakes in hundreds of AI companies. The investor and Trump administration AI and crypto czar is offering rare expertise in government while pushing for decisions that could boost the very firms he profits from.

Is this corruption, or is it just how tech policy works now?

This week, [an investigation by The New York Times](https://sfstandard.com/2025/07/10/david-sacks-crypto-white-house-donald-trump/) concluded that Sacks remains invested in 449 companies with AI products, despite divesting $200 million of his crypto investments and pulling out of Meta, Amazon, and xAI.

ADVERTISEMENT

Sacks “has helped formulate policies that aid his Silicon Valley friends and many of his own tech investments,”[the Times states. (opens in new tab)](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/11/30/technology/david-sacks-white-house-profits.html?unlocked_article_code=1.5E8.2ukB.013v_Gf3Ix79&smid=nytcore-ios-share)

Tech elites had a curt response: Duh.
