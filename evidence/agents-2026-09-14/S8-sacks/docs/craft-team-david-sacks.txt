### David Sacks

Partner

[![Image 7](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/62e5e7d55b5f316055aa3d6c/62e5e7d55b5f319963aa3dac_icon-linkedin.svg)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/davidoliversacks/)[![Image 8](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/62e5e7d55b5f316055aa3d6c/66ced4acc017d0b8f03a0cc7_Group%20(15).svg)](https://twitter.com/DavidSacks)[![Image 9](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/62e5e7d55b5f316055aa3d6c/62e5e7d55b5f311e78aa3e1d_icon-medium.svg)](https://davidsacks.medium.com/)[![Image 10](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/62e5e7d55b5f316055aa3d6c/6497378e3518866550d22961_wikipedia.png)](https://www.craftventures.com/team/david-sacks#)[![Image 11](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/62e5e7d55b5f316055aa3d6c/65e23a767aec65fb1c8b7ed8_substack-icon%20(1).svg)](https://www.craftventures.com/team/david-sacks#)

David Sacks has been a successful founder and investor in Silicon Valley for over 25 years. He has invested early in some of the most iconic companies in tech, including Airbnb, Facebook, Palantir, SpaceX, and Uber.

‍

He started his career in tech as PayPal’s first product leader and COO. Subsequently, he founded enterprise collaboration startup Yammer, which was acquired by Microsoft for $1.2 billion. He then founded Craft Ventures, a venture capital firm based in San Francisco.

‍

In 2025, President Trump named him White House AI & Crypto Czar and Chair of the President’s Council of Advisors on Science and Technology (PCAST).

‍

David is co-host of the All In podcast, which is routinely one of the top podcasts overall, and the #1 tech podcast.
