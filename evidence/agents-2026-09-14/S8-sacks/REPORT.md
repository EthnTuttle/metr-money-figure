# S8-sacks — the accuser's own ties, from the same kind of records

Agent lane S8, 2026-09-14. Fetch-only. Every quote below is verbatim from a saved copy under `docs/`; findings with URLs and dates are in `findings.csv` (41 rows). Facts only; no motive is asserted.

Headline for the infographic: the public record on Sacks consists of two White House Counsel waiver memos (March 5 2025, crypto; June 2025, AI), not an OGE Form 278e. Those memos show Craft Ventures held xAI (0.044% of Sacks's assets) and Sacks held Meta/Amazon/TSMC directly (1.831%), all marked "divestment pending" in mid-2025; neither memo lists Anthropic or OpenAI. ## Gap 1 — Financial disclosure (OGE 278e), waivers, divestitures

**Verdict: 278e NOT FOUND; two primary waiver memos FOUND and read in full for the operative pages.** No OGE Form 278e for Sacks is public. Sen. Warren's May 5 2025 letter to OGE states "because Mr. Sacks's 278e has not been made publicly available, the public cannot verify the waiver's factual claims" and asks OGE to "seek Mr. Sacks's consent to make his confidential financial disclosure form public" — i.e. the Senate letter itself treats the filing as confidential (`docs/senate-banking-oge-sacks-letter.pdf`). Seven probes of whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/…/Sacks-David*.pdf all returned 404; the White House 278e upload set indexed by search (Wiles, Miller, Harp, etc.) has no Sacks entry. VKTR (2025-08-11): "Sacks has stated he is unpaid. Because of that, and the fact that his financial disclosures weren't made public, it's likely he doesn't have to comply with Form 278 regulations." His lawyers' letter, as reported by The Block (2025-12-01), says he "submitted required financial disclosures when joining the administration as a Special Government Employee and received two ethics letters". What is public are those two letters. The **March 5 2025 memo** (11 pp, image-only PDF; read from rasterized pages in `docs/memo-pages/`) grants "a waiver pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 208(b)(1), of any conflict of interest regarding particular matters of general applicability concerning the digital asset industry", records that "you and Craft Ventures have divested over $200 million of positions related to the digital asset industry, of which at least $85 million is directly attributable to you", and sizes what remains as percentages only: Craft Funds' private crypto-company interests "less than 3.8% of your total investment assets" (BitGo <2.5%, Lightning Labs <1.2%), Beldore Capital Fund (S&P 500 exchange fund) 5.07%, AL Venture (AngelList) 0.03%. The **June 2025 AI memo** (39 pp, undated on its face; Axios says posted June 13 2025 and "appears to have been written months ago, since it refers to March 31 as a future date") grants the AI waiver and, in footnote 2, names the holdings: "You have initiated divestment from your directly held interests in Meta, Inc., Amazon, and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC). Your TSMC and Amazon holdings will be fully sold by March 31, 2025. Your Meta holding will be divested no later than the end of Q2 2025. […] Craft Ventures in turn has initiated divestment of its direct interests in X.AI. […] You and Craft Ventures each still hold an insignificant interest in Groq (amounting to less than 0.001% of your total assets) through an independent investment fund." Schedule A: Amazon 0.034%, Meta 1.762%, TSMC 0.035% (total 1.831%, all "Divestment pending"). Schedule B (Craft Funds AI interests, ten names): After Thought AI, Etched, Groq, Paravision, Rime Labs, Runway AI, Slingshot AI, Stability AI (0.001%), Valor Mythic Holdings, and "*X.AI Corp. 0.044%" — total 0.045%. The memo states "you have initiated or completed divestment of over 99% of your holdings in companies that could potentially raise a conflict of interest concern", that Craft Funds divested "over 97%" of theirs with the residue "less than 0.1% of Craft Funds' invested assets", that SaaS interests are "just over 13% of your total assets" (direct SaaS incl. Palantir 1.819%, GlueGroups 0.914%, Houzz 0.829%, Addepar 0.765%), hardware "less than 2%", and Sequoia LP interests 1.2% with a sale closing March 31 2025 plus "approximately 90 other venture capital funds". Limitation: "Craft Ventures agrees to consult with the Office of White House Counsel regarding new potential investments prior to closing". No dollar totals appear in either memo. The NYT (2025-11-30; page blocked, quoted via TechCrunch) counted "708 tech investments, 449 are AI companies" across the filings and said the filings "do not disclose the remaining value of his crypto and AI investments, nor do they say when he sold off the assets he divested"; Sacks replied that he has "no conflicts of interest to uncover".
