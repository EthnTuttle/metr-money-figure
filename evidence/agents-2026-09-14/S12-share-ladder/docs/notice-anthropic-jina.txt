## About Anthropic, PBC

Anthropic develops large language models and related tools for general-purpose AI assistants. Its main product is Claude, which supports conversation, document analysis, summarization, and code generation via apps and an API. The company focuses on research and techniques intended to make AI systems more reliable and aligned with human intent.

Some issuers do not allow transactions. In these cases, buyers will need to purchase indirect interests in the business via special purpose vehicles (SPVs) or forward purchase contracts (forwards).
