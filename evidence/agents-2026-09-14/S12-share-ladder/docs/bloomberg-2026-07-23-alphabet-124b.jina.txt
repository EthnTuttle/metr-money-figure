## **Takeaways** by Bloomberg AI[Subscribe](https://www.bloomberg.com/subscriptions)

The value of [Alphabet Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL:US)’s stake in the artificial intelligence startup [Anthropic PBC](https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/1892140D:US "Click to view webpage.") has soared to around $124 billion, making it one of the most lucrative bets in the company’s history.

The Google parent disclosed in a [filing](https://www.sec.gov/ix?doc=/Archives/edgar/data/0001652044/000165204426000071/goog-20260630.htm "SEC filing") Thursday that the value of its investments in unidentified private companies totaled roughly $124.3 billion as of June 30. The filing notes that this value is “primarily” driven by one investment, which is a reference to Alphabet’s stake in Anthropic, according to a person familiar with the matter, who asked not to be identified because the information isn’t public.
