Join us in understanding the path of AI
We are a multidisciplinary, global, and curiosity-driven team committed to supporting people and organizations worldwide in understanding and navigating the rapid evolution of AI technology.

Expression of Interest: Special Projects Associate
Data Scientist
Epoch is seeking part-time data scientists to assist with our AI research efforts. This role involves reviewing technical literature, tracking benchmark data, compiling technical infrastructure details, and analyzing various sources to build comprehensive insights about AI models, data centers, and companies.
Apply
Researcher / Senior Researcher
Epoch AI is seeking researchers and senior researchers to improve society's understanding of AI capabilities and their trajectories.
Apply
Software Engineer, Benchmarking
Epoch AI is looking for a Software Engineer who will help us evaluate frontier AI models, enabling researchers, developers, and policymakers to better understand AI development. The role will involve running and maintaining our benchmarking infrastructure as well as contributing to the development of brand new benchmarks.
Apply
IT and Security Specialist
Epoch AI is looking for an IT and Security Specialist to own the systems, accounts and access the whole team depends on, and to set the security standards that protect our research and our data. You would decide who can reach what, run the accounts and tools we all work in, find and fix security risks yourself, and work with our engineers and researchers so that our protocols get followed rather than worked around.
Apply
Senior Product Designer
Epoch AI is looking for a Senior Product Designer to lead UI/UX and data visualization at Epoch AI. This role involves managing our design team; translating complex research into intuitive, engaging designs; and setting the direction for how our work is perceived and used by researchers, policymakers, and other stakeholders.
Apply
Social Video Producer
Epoch AI is looking for a founding Social Video Producer who will help us launch short-form video for our new media program. The role will involve producing and editing videos about AI for TikTok and Instagram, and shaping how we show up on those platforms.
Apply
Writer & Editor
Epoch AI is looking for a founding Writer & Editor as we launch a new media team to bring our research to a wider audience. The role will involve pitching, writing, and editing pieces for a general audience as part of our new media program.
Apply
Expression of Interest
Our interdisciplinary team is constantly in search of talent that can add to our skill set, including in research, operations, engineering, data, and communications. Please submit an expression of interest if you are interested in working with us. We won’t scan applications on a regular basis, but we will reach out if there’s an open position we think you might be a good fit for.
