Programs

Finding, connecting, and supporting top talent in AI safety

Constellation’s programs provide time, resources, and structure to help researchers, policymakers, and practitioners make progress on the most critical questions in AI safety.

Constellation's programs provide the time, space, and structure for researchers and strategists to make progress on critical problems.

General Hosting & Visitors

Our Berkeley research center hosts select individuals and teams working on AI safety.

We provide ongoing access to our Berkeley research center for established individuals and organizations in our network whose work aligns closely with our mission and benefits from proximity to our network.

Constellation also offers short-term visits for AI safety and security talent – and for experienced professionals from other industries who are interested in contributing to the field.
Felix Binder, Research Scientist, Meta
“Constellation has been tremendously useful in my transition from cognitive science into AI safety research. I likely would not be where I am today without the support of Constellation's community and the Astra Fellowship.”
Erik Jenner, Research Scientist, Google DeepMind
“Constellation’s Visiting Fellowship was tremendously helpful for refining my views on AI safety and becoming a better AI safety researcher… I’ve learned about many interesting ideas at Constellation that went on to become highly influential, both to the field as a whole and to my own work.”
Alex Mallen, Member of Technical Staff, Redwood Research
“If not for Visiting Fellows, I probably wouldn’t have moved here long-term or gotten a job at Redwood—at least not within the year following Visiting Fellows.”
