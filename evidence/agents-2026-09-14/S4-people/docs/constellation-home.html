    Astra Fellowship
    A fully funded, in-person program that pairs senior advisors with emerging talent on 5 month technical, governance, strategy, and field-building projects.
    Visiting Fellowship
    A lightly-structured 3–6 month funded fellowship for full-time AI safety researchers to connect with the Constellation network and work from our Berkeley research center. 
    Incubator
    Our incubator seeks to build the next wave of organizations working on advanced AI risks by providing funding, operational support, and access to our expert network.
    Generator Residency
    A fully-funded, three-month residency where generalists pitch, build, and ship projects that increase capacity and infrastructure across the AI safety ecosystem.
