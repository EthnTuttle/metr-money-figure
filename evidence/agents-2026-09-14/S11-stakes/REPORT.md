# S11 — Individual investors' stakes in Anthropic: what is actually public

Built 2026-09-14 (fetch-only; WebSearch budget ran out after 10 queries, so the rest was located by direct URL, Wayback CDX, CourtListener RECAP and ProPublica). Rows are in `stakes.csv` (ST01–ST71); every saved page is under `docs/`. Grades: **A** = court/SEC/IRS filing or issuer release; **B** = named-outlet press restating a filing or a named source; **C** = Forbes/Bloomberg estimate or self-statement without numbers; **D** = aggregator/unsourced.

## Headline

Only three Anthropic holders have a public *number* of shares or a public *percentage*, and all three are FTX-related and come from court dockets: Clifton Bay (Alameda) — 44,539,240 Series B Preferred shares, 13.56% fully diluted at Series B close (30 Apr 2022), 7.84% in Jan 2024 — and, by dollar amount only, Caroline Ellison ($10M SAFE) and Nishad Singh ($40M SAFE). Jane Street's 3,332,833 shares bought from the estate are also in a court exhibit. For Moskovitz, Tallinn, Schmidt, McClave and CERR/Macroscopic there is **no** public dollar amount, share count or percentage from any filing, issuer release or self-statement; the only number anywhere is Forbes' "estimated $500 million" for the Moskovitz/Tuna stake (Nov 2025), and Forbes does not show its basis.

## Per investor

**Sam Bankman-Fried / Alameda (Clifton Bay Investments LLC) — calibration case, grade A.** The FTX estate's sale-procedures motion (D. Del. 22-11068, Doc 6952, 3 Feb 2024) states: M-SAFE bought 5 Oct 2021 for $500,000,000; converted 13 May 2022 into 44,539,240 Series B Preferred at $11.2261/share; "approximately 13.56% of Anthropic, on a fully diluted basis" at the 30 Apr 2022 Series B close; "approximately 7.84% … as of January 2024" (ST01–ST05). The executed M-SAFE (Doc 7590-1) carries a $224.5211 M-SAFE price, exactly 20× the conversion price, so a 20-for-1 adjustment happened between Oct 2021 and May 2022 (ST06). Sold in two court-noticed tranches: 29,465,891 shares for $884,109,327 (Doc 10241, 22 Mar 2024, 24 buyers, ATIC largest) and 15,073,349 shares for $452,268,300 (Doc 16380, 31 May 2024, G Squared largest); the two totals sum exactly to 44,539,240 and both price at $30.004/share (ST07–ST08, ST56–ST57). Press (Unchained, CoinDesk, The Block, Business Insider) matches the filings (ST09–ST11).

**Caroline Ellison and Nishad Singh — amounts grade A, everything after that B/C.** SDNY consent forfeiture orders (Docs 510 and 532, scanned; transcribed from page images) each forfeit "the shares of Series B Preferred Stock in Anthropic PBC, acquired … on or about May 13, 2022, through the Simple Agreement for Future Equity" bought for $10,000,000 (Ellison, ~31 Mar 2022) and $40,000,000 (Singh, ~5 Apr 2022) (ST13, ST15). Neither order states a share count. The government took title Feb 2025 (Ellison; Rehn declaration Doc 558) and Apr 2025 (Singh), and Business Insider (31 Aug 2026, one source "familiar with the sale") reports the Marshals sold both blocks to existing Anthropic investors during 2025 at an undisclosed price to undisclosed buyers (ST14, ST16–ST17). The "$4.17–5.03B" / "$2.62B" current-value figures and "$250M–$1.1B" proceeds ranges are UCLA/PitchBook estimates quoted by BI, method not shown (ST18–ST19). If their SAFEs converted at Clifton Bay's $11.2261 (same-price assumption, not in the record) the counts would be 890,781 and 3,563,125 shares; that assumption is flagged in the notes.

**Jane Street — grade A for the 2024 purchase, C for everything since.** Court exhibit: Jane Street Global Trading, LLC, 3,332,833 shares for $99,999,988 (ST20–ST21). Anthropic's own Series E, F, G and H releases list Jane Street as a participant with no amount (ST23–ST26). Bloomberg (24 Apr 2026): its Anthropic stake "was the driving force behind $830 million of third-quarter gains from bets on private firms" (a Q3-2025 mark, not a stake value); Bloomberg 8 May 2026 repeats that it "has a stake" (ST27–ST28). The "$20 billion private-AI portfolio, majority Anthropic" and "50x" figures are from a GeekPark piece republished by KuCoin, unsourced (ST30). Two-number arithmetic only: 3,332,833 × $56.09 = $186.9M at the Series E price, ignoring the undisclosed E–H purchases.

**Dustin Moskovitz (with Cari Tuna) — grade C.** Issuer release: participated in the $124M Series A; amount undisclosed (ST31). Forbes (Phoebe Liu, 7/10 Nov 2025, via Forbes Australia/India syndications): "Their Anthropic stake (worth an estimated $500 million) was moved into a nonprofit vehicle in early 2025 so they could invest any 'significant financial return' back into philanthropy and 'dispel any perception of conflict of interest'" (ST32–ST33). The vehicle is not named. Forbes' own profile ($8.6B, 5 Sep 2026) and Bloomberg's profile (20 May 2026) never mention Anthropic, so neither list publishes an Anthropic component (ST34–ST35). Good Ventures Foundation's 990-PF for FY to 30 Jun 2025 shows "Investments—other" rising from $3.04B to $4.0B but the attached schedule says only "ALTERNATIVE INVESTMENTS"; "Anthropic" does not appear, so the return neither confirms nor excludes Good Ventures as the vehicle (ST36). Longterm Wiki's "0.8–2.5% / $3.0–9.5B" is self-described as an estimate with "no public disclosures" (ST37). Two-number arithmetic: $500M is 0.27% of the $183B Series F post-money or 0.14% of the $350B valuation reported the same month — which base Forbes used is not stated.

**Jaan Tallinn (via Metaplanet Holdings OÜ) — grade C.** Led the $124M Series A and participated in Series B, per issuer releases; no amount (ST38–ST39). Self-statements, relayed by Postimees (22 Feb 2026) from Äripäev: "Between the current Series F and Series A there have been several rounds that substantially diluted Metaplanet's stake"; he declined a board seat and is a board observer; Postimees says he "has not agreed to say how large his stake is" and infers he is a billionaire "at least on paper" (ST40–ST42). Metaplanet's site lists Anthropic as an active 2021 portfolio company, no figures (ST43). Forbes has never carried a Tallinn profile (ST46). Longterm Wiki "0.6–1.7% / $2.3–6.5B" is unsourced (ST44); the Dealroom "$623M post-money / roughly 250x" note could not be fetched (Cloudflare, no Wayback capture) and is recorded only as an unverified search snippet (ST45).

**Eric Schmidt — grade C.** Series A participant per issuer release (ST47). CNBC (21 Jan 2025, Fintrx data): family office Hillspire has invested in 22 private AI firms since 2019 "including Anthropic"; no cheque size (ST48). Forbes profile ($37.8B, 13 Sep 2026) does not mention Anthropic; Bloomberg's profile was not retrievable (ST49). Nothing else public.

**James (Jim) McClave — grade C.** Series A and B participant per issuer releases (ST50–ST51). CNN (10 Mar 2024): early investor in both rounds; he and Emily Berger work at Jane Street and run BEMC 4 Association and BEMC Foundation (ST52). BEMC Foundation's 990-PFs (TY2023, TY2024) hold one "Investments—other" item, a 0.17–0.23% interest in JSHP LLLP (FMV ~$41M); no Anthropic shares, so any Anthropic holding is personal or in another entity (ST53). No amount, count or percentage anywhere.

**Center for Emerging Risk Research / Macroscopic Ventures — grade C.** Series A and B participant per issuer releases; EA Forum topic page confirms "Past investments include Anthropic's Series A and Series B" and ~$30M of 2025 grants (ST54–ST55). No US 990 filer with either name was found on ProPublica, so no investment schedule could be checked. No figures.

## What a defensible estimate for Moskovitz / Tallinn / Schmidt / McClave / CERR would require

1. The Series A price per share and each participant's cheque (Anthropic's Series A certificate of incorporation or the Series A purchase agreement, or a court/SEC exhibit that reproduces them — none is public; the future S-1, if it names 5%+ holders, will only catch those above 5%).
2. Series B cheques for Tallinn, McClave and CERR (Series B was $580M of which $550M is accounted for by Clifton Bay, Ellison and Singh, leaving at most ~$30M across Tallinn, McClave and CERR combined — arithmetic on issuer and court numbers, ST12–ST15).
3. A fully diluted share count at each later date (the 2024 count is solid at 568.1M; 2025–2026 counts depend on convertible-note conversion prices Amazon and Google have not disclosed).
4. Any secondary sales or transfers (Moskovitz's 2025 transfer to a nonprofit vehicle is the only known one; whether Tallinn, Schmidt or McClave sold into the 2024 FTX-era secondaries or the 2025/2026 tenders is unknown).
Without (1) and (2), any percentage for these five is a guess; the Forbes $500M for Moskovitz is the only third-party number and its basis is unstated.
