AI research and products that put safety at the frontier.

Anthropic's work centres on developing AI systems that are not only powerful, but also aligned with human values and ethical principles. Their approach, exemplified by innovations like Constitutional AI, aims to ensure their models prioritize safety, reliability, and beneficial outcomes. By focusing on long-term societal impact and rigorous research into AI safety, Anthropic is working to create advanced AI technologies that can serve humanity responsibly, while addressing both the opportunities and potential risks that such systems present. Their efforts extend beyond just model development to include interpretability research and novel governance structures.
