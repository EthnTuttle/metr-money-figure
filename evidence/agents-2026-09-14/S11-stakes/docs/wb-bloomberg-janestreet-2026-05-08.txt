 May 8, 2026 at 4:15 PM UTC 
 Updated on May 8, 2026 at 4:42 PM UTC 
 Bookmark Save
 Jane Street Group notched a record quarter in the first three months of the year with $16.1 billion of trading revenue, more than double its haul in the first quarter of 2025, as the market maker cements its status at the top of Wall Street’s trading desks. 
 Net income also more than doubled from a year earlier, with Jane Street reporting $10.3 billion in the first quarter, according to people familiar with the matter, who asked not to be identified citing private information.
