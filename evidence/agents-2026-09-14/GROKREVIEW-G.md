Bottom line first. (a) Nothing in these five lanes names the Anthropic vehicle; Q2 stays OPEN. (b) The most consequential new item is on VARA's Form ADV, which the lanes fetched but did not read to the end: **50% of VARA's four clients have invested in VAR AI Fund LP.** The four clients are the master fund, its Cayman feeder and two charitable organizations, one of which is Good Ventures Foundation. The master cannot invest in itself, so at least one of the two charitable clients is a limited partner in the fund — and if the feeder is set aside (as Q19's instruction does for solicitation), both are. This is the first filing-level evidence that a charitable client of the foundation's manager holds the manager's AI hedge fund, which returned +113% in 2025 and was "up nearly 200% through June" 2026. It is not yet an identification of Good Ventures Foundation as that LP. (c) The foundation's own 990-PF payout did not "surge": Part XV paid $306.7M in FY2025, the lowest of four years while assets rose from $4.0B to $10.1B, and for the first time in the series the foundation drew $56.1M of excess-distribution carryover to meet its distributable amount. The $1B GiveWell commitment "in partnership with Good Ventures" exceeds any year's 990-PF payout, so "Good Ventures" money in Coefficient's usage is paid mostly from accounts that are not the 990-PF filer (ST109/ST116 already show NPT and SVCF paying Coefficient's grants to the cent). (d) The Remainder Interest Trust's two gifts account for ≈2.86M of its 6,830,855 Meta Class B shares (≈42%); ≈3.97M shares (≈$2.9B at June 2025) are unaccounted for on the public record, and a gift of them to a donor-advised fund would be invisible by design.
