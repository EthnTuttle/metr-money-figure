 Donate to METR
 Why donate?
 Donations fund our assessment of AI capabilities, risks and mitigations, and are crucial for our ability to pursue groundbreaking projects. We keep our evaluations independent and trustworthy by relying on support from a wide range of independent donors.
 With the support we've received, we've produced work such as:
 Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Tasks
 We found that the length of tasks AI agents can complete has doubled approximately every 7 months for 6 years. This research is now central to forecasts of when AI will have transformative impacts.
 Frontier Risk Report
 A pilot assessment of rogue deployment risk at frontier AI companies, evaluating misalignment risks from AI agents used inside frontier AI developers, with participation from Anthropic, Google, Meta, and OpenAI.
 Measuring the Impact of AI on Open-Source Developer Productivity
 We conducted an RCT and found that experienced open-source developers systematically overestimate how beneficial AI tools are for their productivity.
 View all research
 Independent funding has been crucial for our ability to pursue the most promising research directions and set standards for evidence-based understanding of risks from AI. It is also part of how we ensure that our research is as accurate as possible.
