Donating via every.org
The button above goes to a third party donation processor, every.org
 All donations are unrestricted, even if you give to a specific fundraiser. Every.org re-grants to METR as a batched, unrestricted donation.
 You do not need to tip every.org, they were emphatic about this when asked. They do not charge METR for use of the platform.
